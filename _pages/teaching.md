All my teaching was done at [Sorbonne Université](https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/en).

- **2022/2023, first semester** — Exercise classes in analysis, first year of the Bachelor's programme (54 hours).
- **2020/2021** — Tutoring in measure theory (first semester) and in ordinary differential equations (second semester), second and third years of the Bachelor's programme.
- **2019/2020** — Tutoring in topology and differential calculus (third year, first semester), and in series and series of functions (second year, second semester).

Tutoring sessions are small-group support classes, two hours a week over twelve weeks, held in addition to the regular lectures.
